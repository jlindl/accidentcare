# References: Accident Care homepage

Captured 2026-11-19 with Playwright (Chromium headless) at 1440x900 and 390x844, three screens each. Screenshots are in `/references`.
These are references for technique only. The finished site takes none of their look, layout or copy.

## 1. Mercury (fintech): mercury.com
Source: ogimage.gallery (Finance). Screens: `references/mercury-1440-0.png`, `mercury-390-*.png`
- Centred hero with one plain sentence and one action. The scene behind it sets the mood without needing to be read.
- The regulatory disclaimer sits in a quiet, legible pill at the bottom of the first screen, so compliance reads as confidence rather than small print. **Take:** put our "non-fault accidents only, not a law firm" style truth in the first screen in a calm pill.
- Generous air around the headline, with almost nothing competing.

## 2. Oscar (health insurance): hioscar.com
Source: ogimage.gallery (Health). Screens: `references/oscar-1440-0.png`, `oscar-390-*.png`
- A high-contrast serif display over real human photography, with two actions where the secondary one is "Call to enroll". **Take:** treat the phone call as a first-class button, not a footer link.
- A safety banner about fraudulent calls is shown openly at the top. **Take:** trust signals can be practical and useful, not just badges.

## 3. Ramp (fintech): ramp.com
Source: ogimage.gallery (Finance) and socialproofexamples.com (social proof). Screens: `references/ramp-1440-0.png`, `ramp-1440-1.png`
- A very large, tight-tracked, two-beat statement headline ("X. Y both."). **Take:** a two-beat headline pattern for "Not your fault. Not your cost."
- Two-tone emphasis in secondary headings (dark then muted grey in one sentence). **Take:** use it for section intros.
- A mono, ticker-style data strip in small caps. **Take:** a mono "scope strip" of facts we can stand behind (non-fault only, UK-wide, no win no fee). We will not animate any number through false values.

## 4. Spectrum.Life (health care): spectrum.life
Source: supahero.io (`/hero/spectrum-life`). Screens: `references/spectrum-life-1440-0.png`
- A huge wordmark as the brand moment, then a heavily rounded colour panel holding a centred hero. **Take:** one soft, large-radius tinted surface to hold the signature section, so it reads as a calm space.
- A heavy grotesk display with very tight tracking and a short line length.

## 5. Lemonade (insurance): lemonade.com
Source: socialproofexamples.com (named in its review-video examples). Screens: `references/lemonade-1440-*.png`
- A friendly serif headline and monochrome line illustration of everyday life. It makes a dull category feel human.
- A press-logo strip directly under the hero. **Take:** proof belongs right after the promise. We have no verified reviews or press yet, so our proof slot holds verifiable facts plus a ready-to-fill Google reviews block. Nothing is invented.

## Skipped
- **One Medical** (onemedical.com): CloudFront returned 403 to the automated browser, so it was skipped rather than bypassed.

## Competitor (content map only, not a design reference)
The Accident Group Ltd: theaccidentgroupltd.com. Screens: `references/competitor-*.png`
- Structure: hero with Call and WhatsApp, help list, four service cards, three steps, claim-type tags, two reviews, four FAQs, closing CTA.
- Gaps we will beat: no explanation of why the service costs a non-fault driver nothing; vague eligibility; no "what to do at the scene" guidance; emoji icons; a generic SaaS-blue template look; recovery is mentioned but has no card of its own.
- Its copy is not reused anywhere.
